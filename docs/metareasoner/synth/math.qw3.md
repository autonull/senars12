## §0 — The Thesis

> **A reasoner is a *graded, budgeted, gated Kleisli category of event-sourced transactions*, whose control words form a Kleene algebra, whose admission is a nucleus, whose reflexivity is a filtration, and whose legal instances are the models of an equational theory Φ.**

Every perception, inference, proposal, judgment, action, consolidation, and self-modification is the *same object*: a **transaction** — a typed, budgeted, graded morphism that emits provenance and commits through a single authority. Everything else — drives, substrates, judges, manifolds, towers — is an *enrichment*: a coordinate one may dial without leaving the space.

The design is generated by **five irreducible structures** and **seven axioms**. We state both, then derive dynamics, governance, degradation, and the feasibility theory.

## §1 — Signatures and Sorts

Let **Cog** be the category of cognitive states. The algebra 𝔑 is many-sorted over the following carriers.

| Sort | Name | Structure | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `X, Y` | State | G-graded, event-sourced object | the plant |
| `A, B` | Content | typed term algebra | what is carried |
| `V` | Belief-truth | evidence monoid `(f,c)` | epistemic valuation |
| `D` | Goal-desire | `(d,c)` | teleological valuation |
| `R` | Resource | ordered commutative monoid | budget |
| `G` | Grade | discrete epistemic grade set | firewall |
| `E` | Event | typed, append-only atom | provenance |
| `κ` | Control word | Kleene algebra with tests | scheduling |
| `2` | Verdict | `{admit, refuse}` | gate output |

**Grades.** `G = {ε, τ}` at minimum — **ε** (epistemic / belief) and **τ** (teleological / goal). States are G-graded coproducts `X = ∐_{g∈G} X_g`. All structures below respect this grading.

**Resource monoid.** `(R, +, 0, ≤)` with `R = ℕ^k` — *k* dimensions (cycles, derivations, memory-ops, model-calls, depth, …), **one table, one arithmetic**, equipped with a ceiling `⌈·⌉` and an open-once reset (§4, A3).

**Event monoid.** `E*` is the free monoid of append-only events under concatenation, with neutral `∅`.

## §2 — The Transaction (the atom of cognition)

> **Definition 2.1 (Transaction).** A transaction is a **Kleisli arrow** of the graded budget monad:
> $$\tau : A \rightsquigarrow^{\,g}_{\,r} B \;:=\; A \;\to\; \mathbb{T}_{r}\,B$$
> of **grade** `g ∈ G` and **cost bound** `r ∈ R`. It is the unique unit of cognition: perception, inference, proposal, judgment, commit, action, learning, forgetting, consolidation, and self-modification are all transactions of different *kind*.

> **Definition 2.2 (Transaction record).** Every transaction carries a total signature:
> $$\tau = \langle \mathrm{id},\; \mathrm{corr},\; \mathrm{kind},\; \mathrm{inputs},\; \mathrm{outputs},\; \mathrm{effects},\; \mathrm{cost}\,\nu(\tau),\; \mathrm{grade}\,g,\; \mathrm{trust},\; \mathrm{risk},\; \mathrm{reversibility},\; \mathrm{fallback},\; \mathrm{proof\!-\!obligations} \rangle$$
> where `corr : CorrelationId` threads stimulus → cycle → transaction → outcome (C2).

**Design consequence (B3 / O8).** There are no privileged mutation paths. A perceived datum, a derived belief, a model-proposed rule, a learned strategy, and a tool action are *identical in kind*; they differ only in `kind`, `grade`, `trust`, and `proof-obligations`. This collapses every accidental asymmetry into declared coordinates.

## §3 — The Commit Monad 𝕋 (Single Commit Authority)

The effects of admission, charging, grading, and emission are bundled into one monad.

> **Definition 3.1 (Budgeted monad).** `𝕋 : R → [Cog, Cog]` is a family indexed by resource, with:
> $$\mathrm{ret} : A \to \mathbb{T}_0 A \qquad
> \mathrm{bind}_{r,s} : \mathbb{T}_r A \to (A \to \mathbb{T}_s B) \to \mathbb{T}_{r+s} B$$
> and **weakening** `weak_{r≤s} : 𝕋_r A → 𝕋_s A`.

The binding law makes resource **linear at the type level**: sequencing a cost-`r` computation with a cost-`s` computation yields cost `r + s`. Budget exhaustion is thus a *typing* error, not a runtime surprise.

> **Axiom M1 (Monad laws).** `bind ∘ ret = id = ret ∘ bind`; `bind` is associative.
> **Axiom M2 (Grade law).** `𝕋_r` preserves the G-grading: it never maps a τ-graded component into an ε-graded one except through an explicitly typed, proof-carrying channel.

> **Definition 3.3 (Single commit authority).** There is a distinguished commit morphism
> $$\kappa_{\mathrm{commit}} : \mathrm{Ver}(\mathrm{Candidate}) \to \mathbb{T}\,\mathrm{State}$$
> satisfying the **universal property**: *every durable state mutation factors uniquely through* `κ_commit`.

$$\forall\, w : \text{durable write},\quad \exists !\; \hat w \;\text{with}\; w = \kappa_{\mathrm{commit}} \circ \hat w.$$

**Design consequence (A2 / O8).** “Where does state change happen?” has exactly one answer. The commit surface is a singleton; admission, verification, budget settlement, risk classification, and rollback all live on this one morphism.

## §4 — The Seven Axioms

The theory of valid reasoners is generated by seven equations/laws. Each is a hard constraint in disguise.

**(A1) Gate orientation.** Every gate `j` is **idempotent** (`j ∘ j = j`) and **monotone**, and carries an orientation:
- *fail-closed* ⇒ **interior**: `j(x) ≤ x` (contractive),
- *fail-open* ⇒ **closure**: `x ≤ j(x)` (extensive).

Total admission is the meet `J = ∧_i j_i`. Gates compose; adding a gate never widens admission.

**(A2) Epistemic firewall (grading).** Content is G-graded and every generator is **grade-respecting**. The signature contains **no generator of type `Reward → V`**. Reward acts only on the attention/priority channel of τ-graded content, never on belief truth `V`.

$$(\text{Reward}) \;\not\!\to\; V \qquad\text{(type-level, not policy)}.$$

**(A3) Resource monotonicity + open-once.** `charge` only decreases a budget; a scope is reopened at most once per cycle (`reset ∘ reset = reset`), preventing “a bound wearing a counter.”

**(A4) Provenance fidelity.** Admit-before-write: no state write occurs without a preceding admission event. And **replay is faithful**:
$$\mathrm{replay} \circ \mathrm{log} \;=\; \mathrm{id}_{\text{reachable states}}.$$

**(A5) Boundedness / anytime.** All containers are capacity-bounded; there is a **natural** abort `abort : 𝕋 ⇒ 𝕋` commuting with every stage. No reasoning path is unbounded in time, memory, derivations, or model calls.

**(A6) Control-word well-formedness.** The tick is the action of a **closed KAT term** `κ`; scheduling is data, not hard-coded sequencing.

**(A7) Reflexive filtration.** Self-modification authority forms a filtration
$$\mathcal{F}_0 \subseteq \mathcal{F}_1 \subseteq \cdots \subseteq \mathcal{F}_n,$$
where level `k` may modify only levels `< k`, and **the constitution — the grading, the monad laws, the firewall — lies outside the tower entirely.** No level approves itself.

## §5 — The Six Interacting Structures

The monad 𝕋 is assembled from six structures. We specify each algebraically.

### 5.1 Control algebra — Kleene Algebra with Tests (O3, B1)

Stages are generators; the tick is a KAT expression.
$$(\kappa) \in \mathrm{KAT}(\Sigma_{\text{stages}}) : \quad \cdot\;(\text{sequence}),\; +\;(\text{choice}),\; ^*\;(\text{iteration}),\; p?\;(\text{guard}),\; 1,\; 0.$$

Conditional graphs, parallelism, optionality, and loops are all KAT terms:
$$\kappa = \mathrm{perceive}\cdot\mathrm{attend}\cdot(\mathrm{ready}?\cdot\mathrm{reason}\cdot\mathrm{authorize})\cdot\bigl(\mathrm{hasProducer}?\cdot\mathrm{propose} + \neg\mathrm{hasProducer}?\cdot 1\bigr)\cdot(\mathrm{due}?\cdot\mathrm{learn})^*.$$

**Stage-skipping, branching, and early-exit become data.** The stage graph is loaded, versioned, and revertable — a point in a space of control words, not code.

### 5.2 Resource economy (O4, B5)

Beyond the monoid, a **valuation** turns budget into an economy:
$$\nu : \mathrm{Op} \to R \;\;(\text{cost}), \qquad \upsilon : \mathrm{Op} \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \;\;(\text{utility}).$$

The scheduler is an **argmax under reservation**:
$$\pi \;=\; \arg\max_{\tau \in \mathrm{Enabled}} \;\frac{\upsilon(\tau)}{\,\lambda\cdot \nu(\tau)\,}\quad \text{s.t.}\quad \nu(\tau) \le B_{\text{remain}}.$$

**Reservations** (`reserve` before, `settle` after) prevent silent starvation; **prices** replace uniform truncation with graceful priority. *Anti-goal A2 is discharged here*: every scheduling decision is itself an event (§5.5), so the economy is inspectable.

### 5.3 Gate nucleus (O7, H2)

Gates form an **oriented nucleus** on the lattice of candidates. Untrusted proposers (neural, model, reflex, peer) emit *proposals*; the nucleus is the meet of admission operators. The **proposer/judge** split is an adjunction:

$$\mathrm{propose} \;\dashv\; \mathrm{admit}$$

whose (co)unit is the **judgment manifold** measuring how much of a proposal survives judgment. Tightening the adjunction ⇒ pure symbolic; loosening ⇒ proposal-rich. Trust becomes a *continuous parameter*, not a wiring decision.

### 5.4 Epistemic grading (O1, H1)

Beliefs carry `(f,c)`; goals carry `(d,c)`; the two occupy disjoint grade components. Revision `⋆` is evidence-sensitive and non-idempotent; paraconsistency is retained (contradictions coexist with distinct truth values). The firewall (A2) makes reward-hacking a **type error**, not a policy violation.

### 5.5 Provenance comonad (O2, H3, H6)

Provenance is a **split epimorphism**
$$\mathrm{replay} : E^* \times X_0 \to X \quad \dashv\!\!\!\dashv \quad \mathrm{log} : X \to E^* \times X_0, \qquad \mathrm{replay}\circ\mathrm{log}=\mathrm{id}.$$

Every transaction — *including control and budget decisions* — emits a correlated event. The verifier is **independent**: it shares no engine dependencies, only a transcribed truth table, with drift pinned by test (H7).

> **Bounded provenance (discharges A4).** The event log is itself under AIKR: append-only, but with governed retention, compaction, and snapshot-cache. Audit depth is a *budgeted coordinate*, so provenance never becomes bloat.

### 5.6 Reflexive filtration (O6, H4)

Self-modification is a tower of increasing authority (parameters → strategies → rules → code), each rung producing **proposals** admitted through §5.3 and validated in shadow. **No self-approval** (Löbian obstruction): the approver at level `k` is external to `ℱ_k`. The constitution is immutable from within.

## §6 — Substrate Synergy and Isolation (B4, H8)

> **Definition 6.1 (Substrate coproduct with arbiter).** Substrates — symbolic-uncertain, exact-rewriting, probabilistic, subsymbolic — form a coproduct of signatures
> $$\Sigma = \coprod_i \Sigma_i$$
> mediated by an **arbiter**. Substrates exchange *proposals through the boundary*, never raw state.

**Non-interference law.** An exact equivalence relation (e-graph union, definitional equality) must **never** be induced by an uncertain similarity score:
$$\text{exact}\ \cong \;\;\perp\;\; \text{uncertain similarity}.$$

Isolation is enforced by memory separation plus the arbiter. This is the algebraic form of “hybrid synergy without semantic contamination.”

## §7 — Dynamics: the Tick as a Coalgebra

> **Definition 7.1 (Tick).** One step of cognition is a coalgebra
> $$\delta : X \to \mathbb{T}\,X$$
> given by the action of the control word `κ`. A **run** is the unfold of `δ`, truncated by budget (anytime):
> $$\mathrm{run} = \mathrm{unfold}(\delta)\;\big|\;\text{budget}.$$

In continuous form, the **governed control equation**:
$$\dot X \;=\; \underbrace{\mathcal{T}(X, u)}_{\text{transition under control }u} \;-\; \underbrace{\gamma X}_{\text{thermodynamic decay}} \;+\; \underbrace{\mathcal{E}(I)}_{\text{gated ingress}}$$
where `u` is the control vector selected by §5.2, `γ` the AIKR forgetting, and `ℰ` the admission nucleus filtering stimuli `I`. The epistemic firewall, the single commit authority, and the budget are the *invariants of this flow*.

## §8 — Governance: the Risk–Reversibility Manifold (B6, H10)

Every candidate receives a **governance profile**
$$\Gamma(\tau) = \langle \text{trust},\; \text{confidence},\; \text{risk},\; \text{reversibility},\; \text{blast-radius},\; \text{proof-status} \rangle,$$
and the commit path is a **policy surface** over it:

| trust \ risk | low | high |
|---|---|---|
| **high** | auto-commit | shadow → promote |
| **medium** | provisional (decay) | review |
| **low** | provisional / reject | reject unless strong proof |

Actions are typed by **reversibility**; irreversible effects require classification + authorization. This generalizes autonomy ladders from *actions* to *all cognitive mutation* — context-sensitive governance by construction.

## §9 — Degradation and Failure (C3, H9)

> **Definition 9.1 (Failure policy).** A failure policy is a natural transformation
> $$\eta : \mathrm{Id} \Rightarrow \mathrm{Error},\qquad \eta(X) \in \{\text{degrade}, \text{retry}, \text{abstain}, \text{fallback}\},$$
> composing with transactions.

**Transparency law.** No fault is silently swallowed: every failure is an event. On absence of judgment, compute, memory, tools, or models, the system degrades to a *declared weaker mode* (e.g. pure symbolic), never to undefined behavior, and **never halts cognition on an internal fault**. Fail-closed at the trust boundary; fail-open within.

## §10 — The Feasibility Theory Φ (the hard constraints)

The legal region `D⁺ = { r : Φ(r) }` is cut out by the following, each a theorem of 𝔑. These are the encodings of H1–H10.

| # | Law | Encodes |
|---|---|---|
| **Φ1** | No generator `Reward → V`; ε/τ grading preserved | H1 |
| **Φ2** | Untrusted proposal ⇒ judged ∨ explicitly provisional-typed | H2 |
| **Φ3** | Every mutation ⇒ an event; admit-before-write | H3 |
| **Φ4** | Self-mod ⇒ external/governed approver; no self-approval | H4 |
| **Φ5** | Every path bounded in `R`; natural abort | H5 |
| **Φ6** | Control word is data; scheduling decisions are events | H6 |
| **Φ7** | Verifier ⊥ engine dependencies; transcribed table | H7 |
| **Φ8** | Exact substrate never unions on uncertain similarity | H8 |
| **Φ9** | No silent swallow; faults are events | H9 |
| **Φ10** | Irreversible action ⇒ risk classification + authorization | H10 |

**Coupling entailments** (non-independence of axes): ampliative inference ⇒ graded truth; AIKR ⇒ bounded + forgetting; untrusted proposers ⇒ judge gates; replay ⇒ append-only substrate; code self-mod ⇒ external governance. Points violating Φ are not suboptimal — they are **unstable**.

## §11 — Coherence and Minimality

> **Theorem 11.1 (Compositionality).** Kleisli composition of transactions is associative and unital (M1); budgets add (M2/graded); gates are idempotent (A1); replay is faithful (A4). Hence any two reduction orders of the same transaction net yield **observationally equivalent states** (equal up to event permutation).

> **Theorem 11.2 (Universal property of commit).** `κ_commit` is the unique state-writing morphism; all admission, verification, settlement, and governance factor through it.

> **Theorem 11.3 (Minimality).** 𝔑 is generated by **five irreducible structures** — *substrate, control word, gate, budget, provenance* — and **seven axioms**. Drives, substrates beyond the symbolic, judgment manifolds, manifolds of trust, and the reflexive tower are **enrichments**: coordinates settable from “present” to “absent” without leaving the space.

This is the load-bearing core: strip it further and it is no longer a governed reasoner; add to it and you are choosing a *point* in the space 𝔑 describes.

**Migration path (C4).** Each abstraction replaces one hard-coded mechanism at a time — stages→graph, scopes→economy, gates→nucleus, writes→commit, self-mod→tower — with invariants held fixed at every step. No big-bang rewrite is required; each Φ-law is a regression test at each move.

## §13 — Character of the Design

NOEMA is the point that simultaneously:

- makes **all cognition a transaction** (unification, D3),
- makes **all commitment governed** (B6),
- makes **all resource use explicit** (B5),
- makes **all learning accountable** (C1),
- makes **all state event-sourced** (A3),
- makes **all explanation causal** (C2).

Its bias is elegance-through-unification, bounded always by the epistemic firewall, AIKR, and replayable audit. It gains the power of fluid control, economic scheduling, hybrid substrates, and deep reflexivity **without spending** the guarantees those powers usually consume — because the guarantees are re-anchored in *provenance and graded commit*, not in rigidity.

> **One sentence.** *NOEMA is the least governed coalgebra of budgeted, graded, event-sourced transactions whose control is data, whose commit is singular, whose reflexivity is filtered, and whose legality is the theory Φ.*

That is the complete specification.
